Median makes it easy for merchants to turn their Shopify store into iOS and Android apps. Your store keeps running on Shopify, and Median adds the native features that help you engage customers and grow revenue.

## Key features

- **Push notifications:** Drive engagement and revenue with push notifications from any provider. See [Push notifications](/docs/push-notifications-overview).
- **In-store shopping:** Connect your digital and in-store experience with [QR / barcode scanning](/docs/qr-barcode-scanner), [iBeacons](/docs/ibeacon), and [NFC](/docs/nfc-tag-scanner).
- **Analytics and engagement:** Integrate with platforms such as AppsFlyer, Braze, Cordial, Intercom, and MoEngage. See [Analytics](/docs/analytics) and [Integrations](/docs/integrations).
- **Social login:** Let customers sign in with Google, Facebook, Shop, and Sign in with Apple. See [Using Social Login in your Shopify store's mobile app](/docs/using-social-login-in-the-mobile-app-of-your-shopify-store).

## How Median works

Median converts your Shopify storefront into iOS and Android apps using a WebView approach. Your store runs inside a native "shell" that Median provides. This shell connects your storefront to device features like push notifications, scanning, and biometric authentication.

When you're ready to launch on iOS, you can:

1. Connect your Apple Developer account in Median App Studio
2. Set **Upload to App Store Connect** to **Automatic**
3. Test the uploaded build in TestFlight
4. Submit it for App Review

You don't need to rebuild your store in Swift, build it in Xcode, or upload with Apple Transporter for the standard publishing workflow. If you'd rather have Median's team handle store submission, see the [Publishing Service](/docs/publishing-service).
