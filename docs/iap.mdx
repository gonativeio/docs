## Accepting Payments

To accept payments through in-app purchases, it is critical to follow the Apple App Store and Google Play Store guidelines. Both platforms have strict requirements that determine whether your app must use their respective IAP systems for digital goods and services.

📖 Learn more: [FAQ](/docs/faq-publishing#how-can-i-accept-payments-within-my-app)

## Apple In-App Purchases

Apple mandates IAPs for a wide range of digital content inside iOS apps, covering consumables (like coins), non‑consumables, non‑renewable subscriptions, and auto‑renewing subscriptions. Learn how to implement implement Median's In-App Purchase plugin to offer an end-to-end purchasing flow in your app.

📖 Learn how to set up Apple IAPs: [Apple In-App Purchases](/docs/apple-iap)
