The **Deep Linking Validator** analyzes your domain and checks that `apple-app-site-association` and `assetlinks.json` are set up for deep linking.

It helps verify:

* Files are reachable over **HTTPS** without redirects.
* Responses use the correct **headers** and **status codes**.
* JSON is valid and meets **Apple and Android** expectations.

If your files validate successfully but deep linking isn’t functioning as expected for your app, we recommend posting in our [Discussion Forum](https://median.co/discuss).

Domain to validate: e.g. median.co Validate
