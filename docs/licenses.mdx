Median lets you build and test apps for free. A license is what allows you to publish a white-labeled app to the Apple App Store and Google Play, and it's tied to your app's identifiers. This page covers how licensing works, current plans, what happens to unclaimed apps in App Studio, and how to apply and troubleshoot your license.

## Key facts

- **One license = one app**, published on both iOS and Android.
- **Licenses are tied to your app identifiers**: your iOS Bundle ID and Android Package Name. These must match your listings in App Store Connect and Google Play Console.
- **Building and testing is free.** You only need a paid license to publish without Median branding.
- **Starter licenses are purchased in App Studio** on the **Billing** tab and are applied to your app automatically.
- **For Business, Enterprise, or agency pricing**, [chat with our sales team](#talk-to-sales).

## Pricing overview

There is no cost to build a Median Free app for non-commercial purposes. Free apps can be fully built and tested in App Studio at no charge. When a Free app is published to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, it displays a small "Powered by Median.co" message at launch, and the [JavaScript Bridge](/docs/javascript-bridge) is not available for advanced integrations.

To remove the "Powered by Median.co" branding and unlock full JavaScript Bridge capabilities, purchase a **Median Starter** license directly in App Studio on the Billing tab.

## How licenses work

Each license lets you publish **one app**, which includes both an iOS and an Android version.

### Licenses are tied to your app identifiers

Your license is attached to two identifiers:

- **iOS Bundle ID**, for example `com.example.myapp`
- **Android Package Name**, for example `com.example.myapp`
  Median uses these identifiers to verify that a published app is licensed. They must match:

1. The identifiers set in App Studio
2. Your app listing in **App Store Connect**
3. Your app listing in **Google Play Console**
   If any of these don't match, your app may show as unlicensed during your initial release or a later update. Apple and Google don't let you change an app's identifier after it's published, so choose your final identifiers before applying a license. See [App identifiers](/docs/app-identifiers) for naming guidance.

### Single-platform apps

Median doesn't offer a discounted license for a single platform (iOS-only or Android-only). The platform produces one full-featured app with both iOS and Android versions, and the license covers both.

### Agencies and multiple apps

Each app needs its own license. Development agencies building many apps can get volume-based pricing; [chat with our sales team](#talk-to-sales) for details.

## Building apps without an account

You can start building an app in App Studio immediately, without creating an account first. However, an app that isn't attached to an account is temporary, and App Studio displays a banner showing how much time remains before the app is deleted.

| App age            | Banner shown                                                                                                     | What it means                                                                                                           |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–3 days           | **Draft App:** Verify your email address to create a free account and save your progress.                        | Your app is safe for now, but has no account attached and needs to be claimed to avoid expiry.                          |
| 4–10 days          | **Draft App:** App will be deleted in x days. Create a free account to save your progress and continue building. | _x_ = 10 minus the number of days since the app was created. Create an account in order to keep building.               |
| Older than 10 days | **App Studio Demo Expired:** Verify your email address to create a free account and continue building.           | The app has been expired; you must create an account to keep building otherwise the app will be scheduled for deletion. |

## Applying a license to your app

**Purchased in App Studio:** your app is licensed automatically.

**Purchased directly from Median** (for example, through our sales team): apply the license key you received by email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your identifiers">
    Confirm your iOS Bundle ID and Android Package Name in App Studio match the
    identifiers on your license and your app store listings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your app">
    In App Studio, open the app in your organization that you want to license.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the license">
    Click the **⋮** menu next to your app name and select **Apply Existing
    License**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="App Studio: apply license">
  ![App menu in App Studio with the Apply Existing License
  option](/images/docs/13507f6f612449451fbc9cca0c94347f5796a27e25ebc29b3b256e4b4fc8f6f2-Screenshot_2026-09-09_at_14.05.44.png)
</Frame>

After applying your license, you don't need to rebuild or re-download your app. Just make sure your iOS Bundle ID and Android Package Name are set correctly first.

<Accordion title="Why does my app say it isn't licensed?">
  Almost always, the identifiers don't match. The iOS Bundle ID and Android
  Package Name on your license must exactly match the identifiers you're
  publishing with, both in App Studio and in App Store Connect and Google Play
  Console. Check for typos, casing differences, or a different identifier used
  for one platform. If the identifiers are correct and the error persists,
  contact [Median Support](/docs/support). If a third-party developer built your
  app, confirm they're an approved Median partner and that a valid license was
  issued for your identifiers. See [App identifiers](/docs/app-identifiers).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I build and test before buying a license?">
  Yes. You can build and test your app in App Studio for free, including on the
  simulator. You only need a paid license to publish a white-labeled app without
  the "Powered by Median.co" watermark.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I publish a Free app commercially?">
  Median Free is intended for non-commercial use. Free apps published to the
  stores show a "Powered by Median.co" message and have limited JavaScript
  Bridge and plugin access. Purchase a Starter or Plus license to publish a
  white-labeled commercial app.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to rebuild my app after applying a license?">
  No. Once the license is applied, you don't need to rebuild or re-download your
  app, provided your iOS Bundle ID and Android Package Name were already set
  correctly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a discount for an iOS-only or Android-only app?">
  No. Every license covers one app on both iOS and Android, and there's no
  discounted single-platform license.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I transfer a license to another app or organization?">
  Contact [Median Support](/docs/support) with the license key and the
  destination app or organization. We are happy to transfer the license for you.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
