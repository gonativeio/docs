### Getting started

Before you begin, make sure you have the following:

1. An [Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll) account ($99/year). If you already have one, you can continue.
2. Access to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) with permission to create API keys (Users and Access).
3. Access to the [Median App Studio](https://median.co/organization).

**Tip:** You do not need a Mac, Xcode, or Apple Transporter.

## Median's Publishing Service

Need help publishing your app? Median's Publishing Team has published thousands of apps and is available to help publish your app on your behalf. We offer a 100% money-back publishing guarantee ensuring that your app will be successfully published.

What's included:

* Our experienced team manages the end-to-end process for the Apple App Store and Google Play
* On-device testing for iOS via TestFlight (Apple Mac and Xcode not required)
* Complete configuration of OneSignal push notifications
* Preparation of keystores, signing certificates and provisioning profiles
* Creation of app store listings and screenshots
* Management of app review feedback with guarantee of approval\*
* *You will review your app and all app store data prior to final submission*

All changes to your website will automatically flow through to your app. If an app store update is required due to a change in the native app configuration (e.g. changing the app icon, adding push notifications, update to support latest iOS/Android release, etc), we charge a reduced fee for existing publishing clients.

View our [App Store Publishing Service Steps](/docs/publishing-service) for specific details on the publishing process.

To add App Store Publishing to your app open the **Services** tab when viewing your app on our platform.
