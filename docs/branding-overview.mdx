Mobile apps are a direct reflection of your brand and a cohesive mobile app branding strategy can drive brand recognition, user experience, and retention. This section of the documentation focuses on the different branding options for your app.

  <Card

    in device settings.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Splash Screens"
    icon="image"
    color="blue-500"
    href="https://docs.median.co/docs/splash-screen"
  >
    Configure the screen shown at app launch and when returning from background,
    with static or animated options and light/dark support.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Theme Colors"
    icon="palette"
    color="blue-500"
    href="https://docs.median.co/docs/theme-colors"
  >
    Set the primary colors for text fields and labels (tint on iOS, accent on
    Android).
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Status Bar"
    icon="grip-lines"
    color="blue-500"
    href="https://docs.median.co/docs/status-bar"

    Status bars allow you to customize the color scheme of a user's device
    status bar while your app is in the foreground.
  </Card>
