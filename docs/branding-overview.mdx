Mobile apps are a direct reflection of your brand, and a cohesive mobile app branding strategy can drive brand recognition, user experience, and retention. This section of the documentation focuses on the different branding options for your app.

  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Theme colors"
    icon="palette"

    Set the primary colors for text fields and labels (tint on iOS, accent on
    Android).
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Status bar"
    icon="grip-lines"

    foreground.
  </Card>
