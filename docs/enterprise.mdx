Secure your mobile applications with enterprise-grade security controls and compliance features designed for regulated industries and corporate IT environments. Median.co's enterprise plugins offer advanced capabilities, including jailbreak/root detection, Microsoft Intune integration for MDM/MAM, data protection controls, and network security enforcement.

## Why use enterprise plugins?

- **Compromised device detection:** Identify jailbroken iOS and rooted Android devices at app launch to prevent unauthorized access and protect sensitive data from elevated privileges
- **Mobile device management (MDM):** Integrate Microsoft Intune for centralized device management, app provisioning, and policy enforcement across corporate and BYOD devices
- **Data loss prevention:** Disable clipboard access, block screen captures, and obscure app previews to prevent sensitive information leakage and unauthorized sharing
- **Network security enforcement:** Require HTTPS-only communication by blocking insecure HTTP requests, ensuring all data transmission occurs over encrypted channels
- **Regulatory compliance:** Meet requirements for HIPAA, GDPR, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, and other industry regulations with built-in security controls and audit-ready configurations
- **Granular security controls:** Enable or disable specific security features independently based on your organization's risk profile and compliance needs

## Common use cases

- **Healthcare and HIPAA compliance:** Protect Protected Health Information (PHI) with jailbreak detection, screen capture blocking, and clipboard restrictions for patient portals and telehealth apps.
- **Enterprise workforce apps:** Manage corporate apps on employee devices with Intune MAM, separating personal and work data while enforcing security policies without full device control.
- **Legal and professional services:** Protect confidential client information with screen capture blocking, secure network communication, and clipboard access restrictions for document review apps.
- **Education and student data:** Comply with FERPA regulations by implementing data protection controls and secure communication for apps handling student records and educational content.
