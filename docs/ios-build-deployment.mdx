You need an active Apple Developer account to build and distribute an iOS app. Apple manages provisioning and code signing through App Store Connect.

Most apps never need a Mac or Xcode. You can build your app in Median App Studio, test it in TestFlight, and submit it to the App Store from App Store Connect.

## Build, test, and publish

  >
    Share your app with testers before release. When **Upload to App Store
    Connect** is set to **Automatic**, your distribution IPA is sent to App
    Store Connect and can be added to TestFlight after Apple processes it.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Publish to the App Store"
    icon="apple"
    href="/docs/upload-ios-to-apple-app-store"
  >
    Connect your Apple Developer account with the App Store Connect API, build
    and test your app, then submit the processed build for App Review. Apple
    Transporter is available for manual uploads.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
