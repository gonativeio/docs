A custom URL scheme is a link format unique to your app, such as `youruniquestring.https://example.com/path`. When a user taps one of these links on a device that has your app installed, the link opens the page in your app instead of a web browser.

Custom URL schemes are most useful for:

- **Authentication redirects**, such as returning users to your app after they sign in with a third-party provider.
- **Advanced in-app navigation**, such as linking from another app or a QR code directly to a page in your app.

<Note>
  Custom URL schemes only work on iOS and Android devices with your app
  installed. They don't work in desktop browsers. We recommend this option for
  experienced developers.
</Note>

<Tip>
  For links that you share publicly, such as in emails, text messages, or on
  your website, use [Deep Linking](/docs/deep-linking) (Universal Links and App
  Links) instead. Those links open your website in a browser when the app isn't
  installed, while a custom scheme link does nothing.
</Tip>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a unique scheme">
    Pick a scheme that no other app uses, for example `youruniquestring`. Standard links start with `http://` or `https://`, and your custom scheme replaces that prefix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your links">
    Add your scheme and a dot in front of the full URL of the page you want to open:

    ```text
    youruniquestring.https://example.com/path
    ```

  </Step>
  <Step title="The user taps the link">
    If your app is installed, the device opens your app and loads `https://example.com/path` inside it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Use only lowercase letters in your scheme**

Uppercase letters and numbers can cause links to behave differently on iOS and Android.

</Warning>

## Examples

With `youruniquestring` as your scheme:

| Link                                             | Opens in your app               |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| `youruniquestring.https://example.com/login`     | `https://example.com/login`     |
| `youruniquestring.https://example.com/dashboard` | `https://example.com/dashboard` |
| `youruniquestring.http://example.com/login`      | `http://example.com/login`      |
