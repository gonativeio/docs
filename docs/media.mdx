Add native audio and video playback to your app. Median's media plugins cover background audio playback, live video streaming, real-time video chat, and document capture.

## Why use media plugins?

- **Background audio playback**: Keep audio playing when users lock their device or switch apps, with native controls on the lock screen and notification center
- **Video streaming**: Stream live and recorded video natively with AirPlay and Chromecast support for big-screen experiences
- **Real-time video chat**: Add native Twilio video conferencing
- **Lock screen controls**: Display track metadata, artwork, and playback controls on the lock screen for podcast, radio, and audiobook apps

## Common use cases

- **Podcasts & radio**: Stream audio content with background playback, lock-screen controls, and metadata display for a native podcast or radio listening experience.
- **E-learning & audiobooks**: Enable continuous audio playback for educational content, language learning, and audiobook apps.
- **Live video & OTT streaming**: Deliver live and on-demand video with native streaming and AirPlay/Chromecast support.
- **Telemedicine & video consultations**: Power HIPAA-compliant video appointments, remote patient consultations, and real-time healthcare communication with Twilio video integration.

## Testing audio & microphone in the embedded simulator

Median's embedded App Studio simulator has limited audio support:

- **Audio playback** is not supported in the embedded simulator
- **Microphone input** is not supported in the embedded simulator, so mic access for WebRTC voice/video calls can't be tested in the embedded simulator at all.

To test audio playback, background audio, or microphone-dependent features (for example, the native media player or WebRTC voice calls), build and install your app on a physical device or emulator instead.
