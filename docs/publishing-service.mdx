After purchasing Median's Publishing Service, please review the below and provide our team with the information required. During the publishing process it is important to not make changes to your app on our platform or to make changes to your website. Once our team has been engaged all communication with the app store reviewers must be managed by the Median team.

We look forward to publishing your app soon!

## Process

### 1. Finalize your mobile website

Apple requires a production version mobile app for approval and release to the App Store. Your website should be fully polished (please reference the [publishing guide](/docs/publishing-guide)) and ready for production release. There must not be any indication of a pre-production environment such as "demo", "alpha", "beta", "test", etc.

<Warning>
  **Changes to your website**

Your website structure must not change during Apple's review and approval process (typically 3-5 days).

If there are subsequent app submissions required all changes made to your app on our platform must be made with the involvement of our team.

</Warning>

### 2. Develop your app

_Not applicable for our Business and Enterprise Plans_

Please develop your app in [App Studio](https://median.co/app) and test using the provided browser-based device simulators. You may modify your configuration and rebuild as many times as you wish. Please make special note of any features or configuration options you would like us to complete for you. Once you have finished developing your app, please reply to the publishing email you received when purchasing and include the Private Management URL of the specific app you need published.

Please pay special attention to:

- Overview tab - check your **app name** (at most 13 characters to fit under the app icon on the device home screen, but the app store listing name can be adjusted and made longer prior to submission)
- Branding tab - check your **app icon** on both iOS and Android (recommended square image 1024x1024px)
- Branding tab - Configure **splash screens** for iOS and Android, for light mode and dark mode
- Build & Deploy tab - Specify your **iOS Bundle Id** and **Android Package Name**
- Native Plugins tab - if you have purchased our OneSignal integration and leave the App ID blank we will configure OneSignal for you and enable push notifications on iOS and Android.

### 3. Grant developer access

The easiest way to grant Median access to publish your apps on your behalf is to invite the email provided to you when you purchased our Publishing Service as **Admin** to your Apple and Google developer account teams below.

- Apple: Add to your [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com) team. Learn more in [Apple's guide to adding users](https://help.apple.com/app-store-connect/#/devdbefef011).
- Google: Add to your [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/apps/publish) team. Learn more in [Google's guide to adding users](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/2528691).

If you do not yet have these accounts, you may register them at the following links.

- Apple ($99/year): [Enroll in the Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/). **You must enroll as an Organization, not as an Individual, to publish to the public Apple App Store.**
- Google ($25): [Sign up for Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/apps/publish)

Your Apple and Google accounts must be up to date and ready to accept new apps and app updates. Your Account Holder may need to agree to the latest program agreements which is a requirement to submit your app for release.

### 4. User sign-in

If your app includes any ability for users to sign in, please provide a demo username and password for the review process. Apple will require demo credentials as part of their app store review, and we will need to sign in to generate basic screenshots that you may modify before submission if desired.

### 5. Push notifications setup (only if you have purchased push notifications)

If you leave OneSignal App ID blank in your configuration, we will set up OneSignal and invite you via email. You may then invite additional team members to the OneSignal account as required.

### 6. App Store listing details

Add the below details to your app store listings or provide to our publishing team.

If you would like our team to create app store listing screenshots, please provide instructions for each screenshot required, such as page URLs or steps to navigate to the required screens within your app. If you are providing your own images refer to [App Store screenshots](/docs/app-store-screenshots) for guidelines.

#### Apple App Store

- Primary store page language (Default en-US)

- App Name (30 characters) gets displayed on the store

- Store Category ([App Store categories](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/categories/))

- Age Rating (Default is 4+)

- Does your app display external content? (If Yes you must have any required content rights.)
  - Yes / No (Default no external content is displayed)

- Pricing & Availability (Default is Free and Global)

- Keywords (100 characters, comma separated)

- Description (4000 characters)

- Support URL

- Privacy Policy URL

- Copyright Line (Default YEAR COMPANY NAME)

- If users sign in to the app, Apple requires sign-in credentials
  - Username
  - Password

- App Review Contact Information (Default is Median team contact info, optionally you may manage directly)
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Phone number
  - Email

[Apple App Store listing tips](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)

#### Google Play Store

- Primary store page language (Default en-US)
- App Name (50 characters)
- Short Description (80 characters)
- Long Description (4000 characters)
- Category ([Google Play categories](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859673?hl=en))
- Store listing contact details - Email address (Default will be [info@yourdomain.com](mailto:info@yourdomain.com))
- Does your app contain ads? (Default No)
- Is your app a news app? (Default No - if Yes, provide detailed contact information)
- Pricing & Availability (Default is Free and Global)
- Age Rating (Default is 4+)

[Google Play listing tips](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/4448378?hl=en)

### 7. App Store approval process

Once the Median team has submitted your app for approval **all communication with the app store reviewers must be managed by the Median team**. Our expert publishing team are your representatives with respect to submitting information about your app or appealing any unfavorable review decisions. With our team managing all communications we can be sure there is no room for confusion or inaccurate information provided to the reviewers that could compromise your approval.
