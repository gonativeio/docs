You can also build Android source code for your app directly from the App Studio. To build your app locally, follow the steps in our [Build Android from Source](https://median.co/docs/build-android-from-source) guide. The Android SDK is required which is available as part of the [Android Studio IDE](https://developer.android.com/studio#android-studio-downloads) or for [stand-alone installation](https://developer.android.com/studio#command-line-tools-only).
