## Overview

Google Play requires new apps and updates to be uploaded as an [Android App Bundle (AAB)](/docs/android-app-bundle). You can build a signed AAB directly in Median App Studio and upload it to the Google Play Console, with no Android Studio or local build tools required.

## Upload Workflow

1. Create a [Google Play Developer account <Icon icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" size={12} />](https://play.google.com/console/signup) if you don't already have one.
2. In Median App Studio, open **Build & Deploy**. In the **App Identifiers** section, set your Android Package Name or copy the existing one.
3. Build and download your Android `.aab` file.
4. In the [Google Play Console <Icon icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" size={12} />](https://play.google.com/console), click **Create app** and enter the app name, default language, app or game, and free or paid.
5. Under **Set up your app**, complete the required tasks, such as privacy policy, app access, ads, content rating, target audience and data safety.
6. Go to **Testing → Internal testing**, create a release and upload your `.aab` file. Add testers and start the rollout.
7. When testing is complete, create a release under **Production** with the same or a newer `.aab`, and send it for review.

<Tip>
  Your package name appears in your Google Play URL and cannot be changed after you publish.
</Tip>

For the full set of store listing, testing and review steps, follow the Google section of our [Publishing Guide](/docs/publishing-guide) or see [Google Play Store](/docs/google-play-store).

## Building Locally (Optional)

You can also download Android source code from App Studio and build the app yourself. Follow [Build Android from Source](/docs/build-android-from-source). The Android SDK is required, which is available as part of the [Android Studio IDE <Icon icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" size={12} />](https://developer.android.com/studio#android-studio-downloads) or as a [stand-alone installation <Icon icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" size={12} />](https://developer.android.com/studio#command-line-tools-only).
