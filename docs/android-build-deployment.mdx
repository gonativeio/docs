Building and deploying Android applications requires Android Studio and a structured approach to ensure optimal performance, smooth debugging, and a seamless QA and release process. Whether you're compiling your app from source, configuring localhost testing, managing Android App Bundles, or installing APKs, understanding the core steps in Android build and deployment is key.

## Build, test, and publish

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Install an APK" icon="mobile" href="/docs/install-android-apk">
    Download an APK from App Studio and install it on an Android device for
    testing or for distribution outside Google Play.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Android App Bundle" icon="box" href="/docs/android-app-bundle">
    Build an Android App Bundle (AAB), the format Google Play requires for new
    apps. App Bundles make downloads smaller for your users.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Publish to Google Play"
    icon="google-play"
    href="/docs/upload-android-to-google-play"
  >
    Upload your App Bundle to Google Play Console, test it with testers, and
    release it to the Play Store.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  Use APKs for testing on your own devices and App Bundles for publishing to
  Google Play.
</Tip>
