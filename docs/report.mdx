Median provides the platform and tooling for building and deploying mobile applications. Because Median does not host, control, or moderate the content displayed within third-party apps created using our tools, we rely on our community to help maintain a safe ecosystem.

For your security, we always recommend downloading applications exclusively from official marketplaces (such as the Apple App Store or Google Play Store) and carefully reviewing requested permissions before installation.

<Info>
  **Safety tip**

Only install applications from official sources like the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Always audit application permissions prior to installation.

</Info>

Median is committed to maintaining a safe, legal, and respectful environment for developers and end-users alike. If you encounter an application built on our platform that contains abusive, harmful, illegal, or inappropriate content, please report it immediately using our transparent process.

## How to submit a report

To flag a problematic application, complete our [abuse report form](https://median.co/report).

Provide as much detail as possible including the app name, platform, and specific examples or screenshots of the violation to help our team expedite the investigation.

### Post-submission review process

Once a report is submitted, it undergoes the following lifecycle:

1. **Internal review**: Our Compliance Team will assess the report and verify the issue.
2. **Mitigation and action**: If a violation is confirmed, Median will take appropriate administrative action, which may include suspending the application and associated user account(s).
3. **Follow-up**: If critical details are missing, our team may reach out to you via email to request additional information.

<Info>
  **Confidentiality notice**

All abuse submissions are treated with strict confidentiality. Median will not share your personal information or identity with the reported party without your explicit consent, except where required by law.

</Info>
