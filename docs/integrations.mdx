Extend your mobile app's capabilities by connecting to powerful third-party platforms and services. Median.co's Integration plugins enable seamless connections to subscription management, deep linking, ad monetization, marketing automation, CRM systems, and specialized service providers. Whether you're managing subscriptions with RevenueCat, optimizing user acquisition with Branch.io, or monetizing with AdMob, these integrations eliminate complex SDK implementations and accelerate time-to-market for feature-rich mobile applications.

## Why use integration plugins?

* **Subscription management made simple:** Leverage RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions across iOS and Android without wrestling with StoreKit and Google Play Billing APIs
* **Ad revenue optimization:** Monetize your app with Google AdMob's native banner and interstitial ads, powered by machine learning for maximum earnings
* **CRM and customer data:** Sync user data with Salesforce, Intercom, and other CRM platforms to provide seamless customer experiences
* **Accelerated development:** Skip months of custom integration work with pre-built, tested connections to industry-leading platforms

## Common use cases

* **Subscription apps:** Manage recurring revenue with RevenueCat's unified subscription infrastructure for news, fitness, productivity, and content apps across iOS, Android, and web.
* **Content and media:** Monetize free content with AdMob native ads while maintaining user experience, integrate video platforms like Twilio and Zoom for live streaming.
* **Marketing and growth:** Run sophisticated campaigns using Braze, Klaviyo, or MoEngage for segmented push notifications, email automation, and behavioral triggers.
* **Enterprise and B2B:** Connect Microsoft Intune for mobile device management, Auth0 for enterprise SSO, and Salesforce for CRM data synchronization.

## Getting started

1. **Choose your plugin:** Browse the integration plugins below and select the platform that fits your needs. RevenueCat is ideal for subscription management, Branch.io for deep linking and attribution, and AdMob for ad monetization.

2. **Review documentation:** Read the plugin's documentation to understand API keys, configuration requirements, and platform-specific setup steps for iOS and Android.

3. **Enable in app studio:** Navigate to **Native Plugins** in your Median App Studio, click the **+** button next to your chosen plugin, and enter required credentials (API keys, domain settings, etc.).

4. **Implement with JavaScript bridge:** Add JavaScript Bridge commands to trigger integration functionality. For example:

   * `median.revenueCat.configure()` to initialize subscription management
   * `median.revenueCat.purchase()` to process in-app purchases
   * `median.branch.getLatestParams()` to retrieve deep link data
   * `median.admob.showBanner()` to display ad units
