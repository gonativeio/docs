Analytics plugins provide important insights into how users interact with your mobile app, enabling you to make data-driven decisions that drive growth, engagement, and revenue. By tracking user behavior, measuring campaign performance, and monitoring app health, analytics tools help you understand what’s working and what needs improvement. Median's analytics plugins integrate with platforms like Google Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Meta App Events, and more.

## Why use analytics plugins?

- **Native event tracking**: Capture app installs, launches, and custom in-app actions
- **Marketing attribution**: Connect ad spend to conversions and user lifetime value
- **User insights**: Understand behavior patterns, retention, and engagement
- **Crash monitoring**: Detect and fix issues before they impact users

## Common use cases

- **E-commerce**: Purchase tracking, cart abandonment, product analytics
- **Marketing**: Campaign attribution, channel optimization, ROAS measurement
- **Retention**: Cohort analysis, churn prediction, engagement scoring
- **Stability**: Crash reporting, error tracking, performance monitoring

## Featured plugins

**Not sure where to start? Check out our featured plugins below:**

- **Google Firebase Analytics**: Free, comprehensive analytics with real-time reporting
- **AppsFlyer**: Marketing attribution and ROI tracking across ad campaigns
- **Meta App Events**: Optimize Facebook/Instagram ads and build custom audiences
