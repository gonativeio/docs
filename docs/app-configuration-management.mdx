Good app configuration and management keeps your builds consistent and your deployment pipeline scalable. By managing app identifiers, handling multiple app instances, and integrating CI/CD workflows, you can reduce errors in both Android and iOS development.

## Key aspects of app configuration & management

  <Card

    across development, staging, and production environments. Properly

    prevents conflicts in app installations.

  <Card

    configuration of your app. Using this configuration you can review and/or
    manually edit the full app configuration.

  <Card

    Google Services for Mobile Apps provide essential tools that enhance
    functionality, user engagement, and app performance. From analytics and
    crash reporting to authentication and cloud messaging, these services
    integrate into your app.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Backup management"
    icon="cloud"

  <Card

    versions, A/B testing, and debugging. Developers can configure apps to

    bundle identifiers.

    workflows.
  </Card>

## Best practices for app configuration

- Use environment-specific app identifiers to separate development, testing, and production builds.
- Configure multiple instances with unique URLs, app icons, and app identifiers.
- Automate builds and deployments with a CI/CD pipeline.
- Use Median's backup and configuration management to keep apps consistent.
